#Data
This folder documents the public GEO datasets used in this project.

##GSE103228

Used to assess hsa-miR-873-5p expression in normal brain and glioblastoma samples.

- Normal brain samples: 5
- Glioblastoma samples: 5
- Platform: GPL18058
- Source: NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)

##GSE90886

Used for independent expression assessment of the 17 computationally predicted candidate target genes.

- Normal samples: 9
- Glioblastoma samples: 9
- Platform: GPL15207
- Source: NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)

Raw dataset files are not duplicated in this repository; the GEO accession numbers provide access to the original public datasets.
